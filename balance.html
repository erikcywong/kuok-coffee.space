A US$14.4bn Asset Base Earning US$112m — the Balance Sheet Is Not the Problem

144亿美元资产只赚1.12亿美元 —— 问题不在资产负债表

Unlike a distressed property group, Shangri-La's balance sheet is growing, not shrinking: total assets reached US$14.43bn in FY2025 (+6.9% YoY) and cash and bank balances stand at US$2,530m with US$759m of undrawn committed facilities. The funding cost has been managed down to 4.0% (FY2024: 4.5%) — below the prevailing Fed funds rate. But liabilities have grown faster than assets for six consecutive years, taking liabilities-to-assets from 48.5% (FY2017) to 61.2% (FY2025) on US$7.65bn of interest-bearing debt, while net assets per share has drifted from US$1.84 to US$1.50. The equity story is not solvency — it is the 2.1% return on capital and a 84% discount to the company's own fair-value-adjusted NAV.

与陷入困境的物管集团不同，香格里拉的资产负债表在扩张而非收缩：FY2025总资产达144.3亿美元（同比+6.9%），现金及银行结余25.3亿美元，另有7.59亿美元未提取承诺额度。融资成本已压降至4.0%（FY2024为4.5%），低于当期联邦基金利率。但负债已连续六年快于资产增长，资产负债率从48.5%（FY2017）升至61.2%（FY2025），有息债务达76.5亿美元；同期每股净资产从1.84美元滑落至1.50美元。这并非偿付能力故事——而是2.1%的资本回报率，以及对公司自身按公平值重列后NAV高达84%的折让。

Analyst Read分析师解读

0.34× book, 0.16× adjusted NAV

0.34倍账面，0.16倍经调整NAV

At HK$4.00 the shares trade at HK$14.3bn (US$1.84bn) market capitalisation — 34% of book NAV of HK$11.70 per share, and just 16.5% of the company's own fair-value-adjusted NAV of HK$24.26 per share (hotel properties marked from historic cost to fair value, lifting net assets from US$5,324m to US$11,067m). The market is applying a 66% discount to stated book and an 84% discount to the company's own appraisal. A 0.17%-of-assets agri programme cannot close that gap; a credible new return stream can start the argument.

股价4.00港元对应市值143亿港元（18.4亿美元）——为每股账面NAV 11.70港元的34%，更仅为公司自身按公平值重列的每股NAV 24.26港元的16.5%（酒店物业由历史成本重估至公平值，净资产从53.24亿升至110.67亿美元）。市场对报表账面折让66%，对公司自身估值折让84%。一项占资产0.17%的农业计划无法弥合这一差距；但一条可信的新回报流可以让这场辩论开始。

Funding is already being optimised — that lever is spent

融资优化已在推进——这条杠杆已用尽

Average funding cost fell to 4.0% from 4.5%, treasury management kept it below the Fed funds rate, and net gearing improved to 77.2% from 81.3%. But on US$7.65bn of interest-bearing debt, every 100bp is ≈US$76.5m — several times the entire proposed agri capex. Further repricing needs a better credit story, not a better treasury desk: measurable carbon and traceability data is precisely the kind of asset green and sustainability-linked lenders price.

平均融资成本从4.5%降至4.0%，资金管理使其低于联邦基金利率，净负债率由81.3%改善至77.2%。但面对76.5亿美元有息债务，每100个基点约合7,650万美元——数倍于本计划全部农业资本开支。进一步降息需要更好的信用叙事，而非更好的资金部门：可计量的碳与溯源数据，正是绿色与可持续发展挂钩贷款人愿意定价的资产类型。

The dividend is the tell

股息是最诚实的信号

Management restored HK15 cents and held it through two years of falling profit — a payout of ≈US$70m, about 61% of FY2025 attributable profit and roughly 3.75% yield at HK$4.00. Kuok Hui Kwong, chairman and group CEO since August 2025, has paired that with asset-light expansion: Shangri-La Signatures launch, the Hongqiao airport dual-brand debut and a Wuxi HMA signing. The Group is choosing dividends plus fee-based growth over balance-sheet expansion — which is exactly why a high-ROE, capital-light agri layer fits the current capital-allocation stance.

管理层恢复15港仙并在利润连续两年下滑时维持派息——总额约7,000万美元，约占FY2025归母溢利的61%，按4.00港元计股息率约3.75%。自2025年8月起兼任主席与集团CEO的郭惠光，同时推进轻资产扩张：香格里拉心玺发布、虹桥机场双品牌首秀、无锡管理协议签约。集团选择"分红＋管理费增长"而非资产负债表扩张——这正是高ROE、轻资产农业层与当前资本配置立场高度契合的原因。
