Six Structural Benefits — and the One Link a US$14.4bn Balance Sheet Cannot Buy

六大结构性收益 —— 以及144亿美元资产负债表买不到的那一环

FY2025 was resilient, not transformative. Revenue reached US$2,234.1m (+2.2%), EBITDA US$520.9m (+3.4%), operating PATMI US$122.8m (+6.0%), free cash flow US$313m (+14.8%), RevPAR US$111 (+3%) and gross margin 55.7% — a genuinely high-quality hotel business. Yet return on equity is 2.14% on a US$14.43bn asset base and the shares trade at 34% of book and 16.5% of the company's own fair-value-adjusted NAV. The gap is not operational; it is the absence of a capital-light, high-return profit pool. Kuok Group owns the sourcing, processing, distribution, land and captive demand of the entire coffee chain — everything except the plant.

FY2025是稳健之年，而非变革之年。收入22.341亿美元（+2.2%）、EBITDA 5.209亿美元（+3.4%）、营运归母溢利1.228亿美元（+6.0%）、自由现金流3.13亿美元（+14.8%）、RevPAR 111美元（+3%）、毛利率55.7%——这是一门质地优良的酒店生意。但净产收益率（ROE）仅2.14%（资产基数144.3亿美元），股价为账面的34%、仅为公司自身按公平值重列NAV的16.5%。差距不在经营层面，而在于缺少一个轻资产、高回报的利润池。郭氏集团拥有整条咖啡产业链的采购、加工、分销、土地与内嵌需求——唯独不拥有那株植物。

5.1

Six Positive Developments for the Group对集团的六大正向发展

🌐 1 · A demand driver decoupled from RevPAR

🌐 1 · 一条与RevPAR脱钩的需求引擎

Group RevPAR was US$111 in FY2025 — up 3% from US$108 in both FY2023 and FY2024, three years of near-flat pricing. Mainland China ADR only stabilised in Q4 2025 after a prolonged correction from the 2023 peak, and the company's own FY2025 outlook flags potential disruption to Europe and the Middle East from geopolitical conflict. Coffee seedling demand is climate-driven — ICO projects up to −50% of suitable coffee land by 2050 — structurally uncorrelated with guest arrivals, ADR or regional travel politics.

集团FY2025 RevPAR为111美元——较FY2023、FY2024的108美元升3%，三年价格近乎持平。中国内地平均房价在经历自2023年峰值以来的长期回调后，直至2025年四季度才趋稳，而公司自身在FY2025展望中亦指出欧洲与中东可能受地缘冲突扰动。咖啡幼苗需求由气候驱动——ICO预测2050年适宜种植面积或减少50%——与客流量、平均房价及区域出行环境结构性无关。

📈 2 · The metric that matters is marginal return, not blended margin

📈 2 · 关键指标是边际回报，而非整体毛利率

Group gross margin is already 55.7% — a 73.5%-margin layer at 8–10% of revenue moves the blended figure by well under one point. That is not the prize. The prize is return on incremental capital: the group earns 2.14% ROE on US$14.43bn, while a US$25m programme at steady state generates ≈US$26m a year — a marginal return that is roughly 100× the return on the existing asset base. Small absolute profit, decisive marginal arithmetic.

集团毛利率本已55.7%——一层占营收8–10%、毛利率73.5%的业务，对整体毛利率的拉动不足一个百分点。这不是关键。关键是增量资本回报：集团以144.3亿美元资产取得2.14%的ROE，而一项2,500万美元的计划在成熟期每年产生约2,600万美元——其边际回报约为现有资产基数回报的100倍。绝对利润不大，边际算术却是决定性的。

☕ 3 · 106 hotels, 42,500 rooms: a captive market, not a channel

☕ 3 · 106家酒店、4.25万间客房：是内嵌市场，不只是渠道

FY2025 year-end estate: 106 operating hotels, 42,500 rooms — 87 owned/leased (36,100 rooms) plus 19 managed (6,400 rooms) — across Shangri-La (90), JEN (9), Kerry Hotels (3), Traders (3) and the new Shangri-La Signatures (1), plus 3 owned and 5 managed hotels under development. F&B is the highest-margin revenue line inside a hotel, and "continue to elevate F&B offerings" is a stated pillar of the FY2025 strategy. House-brand coffee is the lowest-capex way to execute it: every F&B director is a buyer already inside the perimeter, so customer-acquisition cost on the first tranche of output is close to zero.

FY2025年末组合：营运中酒店106家、客房4.25万间——自有／租赁87家（3.61万间）加管理19家（0.64万间）——涵盖香格里拉（90家）、JEN（9家）、嘉里酒店（3家）、盛贸（3家）与全新香格里拉心玺（1家），另有3家自有及5家管理酒店在开发中。餐饮是酒店内毛利率最高的收入项，而"持续提升餐饮体验"正是FY2025战略明确列出的支柱之一。自有品牌咖啡是执行该战略资本开支最低的方式：每一位餐饮总监都是已身处体系内的买家，首批产出的获客成本接近于零。

🌿 4 · Green refinancing on a US$7.65bn debt stack

🌿 4 · 为76.5亿美元债务打开绿色再融资

Every 100bp on the interest-bearing debt stack is ≈US$76.5m — several times the entire proposed agri capex. But the treasury lever is largely spent: average funding cost is already down to 4.0% from 4.5%, below the prevailing Fed funds rate. Further repricing needs a better credit story, not a better treasury desk. Measurable per-seedling carbon and traceability data is exactly the asset type green and sustainability-linked lenders price — and the group's 1,273.6k sqm of office plus 899.9k sqm of commercial investment property adds a green-building certification adjacency.

有息债务上每100个基点约合7,650万美元——数倍于本计划全部农业资本开支。但资金端的杠杆已大致用尽：平均融资成本已从4.5%降至4.0%，低于当期联邦基金利率。进一步降息需要更好的信用叙事，而非更好的资金部门。可计量的每株碳足迹与溯源数据，正是绿色与可持续发展挂钩贷款人愿意定价的资产类型——而集团127.36万平方米办公物业与89.99万平方米商用投资物业，又提供了绿色建筑认证的相邻场景。

🧪 5 · IP royalties & carbon credits

🧪 5 · IP特许权与碳信用

Tissue-culture licensing into Wilmar's plantation and commodity counterparties creates royalty income with zero incremental capex and zero depreciation; per-seedling carbon-footprint data creates creditable, reportable ESG assets. Both are the earnings profile a P&L carrying US$398m of EBITDA-to-operating-PATMI gap (largely depreciation and finance costs) most needs: cash-generative, non-cash-charge-free, and reported as a separate segment.

向丰益国际的种植园与大宗商品交易对手方授权组培技术，产生零增量资本开支、零折旧的特许权收入；每株咖啡的碳足迹数据形成可信用化、可披露的ESG资产。EBITDA与营运归母溢利之间存在约3.98亿美元差额（主要为折旧与融资成本）的损益表，最需要的正是这种收益形态：现金创造型、无重非现金费用，且可独立分部列报。

🏨 6 · Dormant space becomes productive — and land monetisation is already proven

🏨 6 · 闲置空间变生产力 —— 且土地变现能力已被验证

Podiums, rooftops and back-of-house areas across 87 owned/leased hotels host aeroponic modules without land acquisition. And the group has already shown it can convert property into profit: the Property Development for Sale segment's effective-share revenue rose to US$85.6m in FY2025 from US$48.5m, with effective-share EBITDA nearly tripling to US$35.0m from US$11.8m. Growing coffee on already-owned square metres is the same discipline applied to a crop rather than a condominium.

87家自有／租赁酒店的裙楼、天台与后场区域可承载雾培模块，无需购地。而集团已证明其可将物业转化为利润：供出售的发展物业分部实际应占收入由FY2024的4,850万美元增至FY2025的8,560万美元，实际应占EBITDA更由1,180万美元近乎三倍增至3,500万美元。在已有平方米上种植咖啡，与开发住宅是同一种资产纪律，只是产物换成了作物。

5.4

Expert Conclusion & Transformation Path专家结论与转型路径

Diagnosis

诊断

Shangri-La's decade divides cleanly: roughly US$1.0bn of attributable profit earned across FY2016–FY2019, then a pandemic trough of US$9.1bn of lost revenue and four loss years, then a genuine but incomplete recovery. FY2025 topline of US$2,234.1m is 88.7% of the FY2018 peak, and the operating business is measurably better — EBITDA +3.4%, operating PATMI +6.0%, free cash flow +14.8%, funding cost down to 4.0%. But three things have not recovered: return on equity (2.14%), net assets per share (US$1.50 versus US$1.84 at the FY2017 peak) and the equity multiple (0.34× book, 0.165× the company's own adjusted NAV). Meanwhile gearing has risen six consecutive years to 61.2% liabilities-to-assets. The portfolio is high-quality; the capital allocation has no engine that earns a high return on new money. Kuok Group is the only conglomerate that already owns sourcing, processing, distribution, land and captive demand across the coffee chain — and owns none of the plant.

香格里拉的十年泾渭分明：FY2016–FY2019累计赚取约10亿美元归母溢利，随后经历疫情期间约91亿美元营收损失与四个亏损年度，再到一场真实但未完成的修复。FY2025营收22.341亿美元，相当于FY2018峰值的88.7%，且经营层面确有改善——EBITDA +3.4%、营运归母溢利+6.0%、自由现金流+14.8%、融资成本降至4.0%。但有三项未修复：净产收益率（2.14%）、每股净资产（1.50美元 vs FY2017峰值1.84美元）、以及股权倍数（账面0.34倍、公司自身经调整NAV的0.165倍）。同期负债率连续六年攀升至61.2%。组合质地优良，但资本配置缺少一台能在新增资金上赚取高回报的引擎。郭氏集团是唯一已拥有咖啡产业链采购、加工、分销、土地与内嵌需求的集团——却唯独不拥有那株植物。

Recommended path (in priority order)

建议路径（按优先级）

1️⃣ Continue the asset-light expansion and return-focused CAPEX discipline already stated in the FY2025 strategy — Shangri-La Signatures, the Hongqiao dual-brand debut, the Wuxi HMA and the management-contract pipeline
2️⃣ Launch Phase 0: a US$1.0m aeroponic pilot plus tissue-culture bench on existing hotel podium space — 0.007% of total assets, funded from US$313m of free cash flow, no land and no leverage
3️⃣ On validated milestones (germination rate, unit cost, audited margin), execute Phases 1–2: house-brand coffee across the 106-hotel estate, then ≈30 farms in Greater Bay Area, ASEAN and Middle East corridors
4️⃣ Route seedling supply, roasting and packaging through Wilmar / FFM counterparties; move output on KLN's 59-country cold-chain network; license tissue-culture IP for royalty income
5️⃣ Feed per-seedling carbon and traceability data into disclosures and refinance toward green / sustainability-linked instruments on the US$7.65bn debt stack
6️⃣ Report the agri segment separately from day one and milestone-gate each phase — then let the 84% discount to the company's own NAV argue with itself

1️⃣ 延续FY2025战略已明确的轻资产扩张与"以回报为导向"的资本开支纪律——香格里拉心玺、虹桥双品牌首秀、无锡管理协议及管理合同储备
2️⃣ 启动Phase 0：在现有酒店裙楼空间投入US$100万雾培试点＋组培实验台——占总资产0.007%，资金来自3.13亿美元自由现金流，不购地、不加杠杆
3️⃣ 里程碑验证通过后（出苗率、单株成本、经审计毛利率）执行Phase 1–2：106家酒店全面启用自有品牌咖啡，再于大湾区、东盟与中东走廊建设约30个农场
4️⃣ 种苗供应、烘焙与包装经丰益／FFM体系流转；产出经嘉里物流覆盖59国的冷链网络流通；授权组培IP获取特许权收入

6️⃣ 农业分部首日即独立列报、逐阶段设里程碑门槛——让对公司自身NAV 84%的折让开始自我修正

One-line verdict

一句话总结

For Kuok Group, aeroponic coffee is not a diversification bet — it is the only missing link in a chain the group already owns end to end. US$25m, or 0.17% of assets and 6.3% of a single year's operating cash flow, buys the plant: a 73.5%-margin layer with a 2–3 year payback, sold through 106 captive hotels, supplied through Wilmar's channels, moved on KLN's 59-country network, instrumented by K2 Strategic's telemetry, and used to reprice US$7.65bn of debt through green finance. The hotels are fine. The missing link is the plant.

对郭氏集团而言，雾培咖啡不是多元化下注——它是一条集团已端到端拥有、却唯独缺失的那一环。2,500万美元，相当于总资产的0.17%、单一年度经营现金流的6.3%，买到那株植物：一层毛利率73.5%、回收期2–3年的盈利，通过106家内嵌酒店销售、经丰益体系供应、沿嘉里物流59国网络流通、由K2 Strategic遥测体系计量，并用以通过绿色融资重定价76.5亿美元债务。酒店业务本身并无问题，缺的是那株植物。
