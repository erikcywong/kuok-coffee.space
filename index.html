Kuok Group · Est. 1949创立于1949年 Shangri-La Asia · 00069.HK · FY2016–FY2025 · FYE 31 Dec财年截至12月31日 Aeroponic Coffee × Tissue Culture

The Only Conglomerate That Already Owns the Whole Coffee Chain — Except the Plant

唯一已拥有整条咖啡产业链的集团 —— 只差那株植物

Kuok Group spans six continents across properties, logistics, hospitality, food & agribusiness, maritime and digital infrastructure. Its hospitality flagship, Shangri-La Asia (00069.HK), has recovered its topline — FY2025 revenue of US$2.23bn is 89% of the FY2018 peak — yet group ROE sits at just 2.1% on a US$14.4bn asset base, and the shares trade at 0.34× book. This report deconstructs Shangri-La's ten-year trajectory and evaluates whether a US$25m aeroponic coffee programme adds the one asset the Group does not yet own: the plant — and with it a capital-light, climate-driven, 73.5%-margin earnings layer sold through 106 captive hotels.

郭氏集团业务遍及六大洲，涵盖地产、物流、酒店、食品农业、海事与数字基础设施。其酒店旗舰香格里拉亚洲（00069.HK）收入已修复——FY2025营收22.3亿美元，为FY2018峰值的89%——但集团ROE仅为2.1%（资产基数144亿美元），股价对账面折让66%（0.34倍PB）。本报告拆解香格里拉十年轨迹，并评估：一项2,500万美元的雾化水培咖啡计划，能否补上集团唯一缺失的一环——那株植物，从而带来一层轻资产、气候驱动、73.5%毛利率的盈利，并通过106家自有酒店直接变现。

02

Executive Summary核心结论

📉 Where the Group Stands: Recovered Topline, Unrepaired Returns

📉 集团现状：收入已修复，回报未修复

Revenue is back to US$2.23bn, but the decade tells a harder story: US$11.3bn of cumulative revenue across FY2016–FY2025 produced cumulative attributable profit of only US$0.2bn, after four loss years (FY2020–FY2023 interims aside) wiped US$9.1bn of the pandemic period alone. FY2025 attributable profit fell 30.4% to US$112m purely on non-operating items — investment-property fair-value gains collapsed 76% and a US$30.4m UK hotel impairment was booked — while operating PATMI actually rose 6.0% to US$122.8m and EBITDA reached US$520.9m. The problem is not the P&L; it is the 2.1% return on a US$14.4bn asset base. Gearing has climbed six straight years to 61.2% liabilities-to-assets.

收入已回到22.3亿美元，但十年故事更严峻：FY2016–FY2025累计营收约113亿美元，累计归母溢利仅约2亿美元。FY2025归母溢利同比降30.4%至1.12亿美元，全部来自非营运项目——投资物业公平值收益锐减76%、并就英国一家酒店计提3,040万美元减值——而营运归母溢利实为增长6.0%至1.228亿美元，EBITDA达5.209亿美元。问题不在损益表，而在144亿美元资产基数上仅2.1%的回报率。负债率已连续六年攀升至资产负债率61.2%。

🧬 The Recommendation: US$25m, Three Phases, 0.23% of Adjusted NAV

🧬 投资建议：三阶段合计2,500万美元，占经调整NAV 0.23%

Deploy ≈HK$195m (US$25m) over five years into aeroponic coffee farms and a tissue-culture lab — 0.17% of total assets, funded from operating cash flow (US$394m in FY2025) and asset-recycling proceeds. Unit model: US$414k per farm, 615k seedlings/yr, US$2.26m revenue at a 73.5% gross margin, payback 2–3 years, IRR ≈25–35%. At steady state the segment could contribute ≈US$26m of net profit a year — roughly 23% of FY2025 group attributable profit — from capital equal to 0.17% of the balance sheet.

五年分期投入约1.95亿港元（US$2,500万）建设雾培咖啡农场与组培实验室——占总资产0.17%，资金来自经营现金流（FY2025达3.94亿美元）与资产循环所得。单农场模型：投入US$41.4万，年产61.5万株，年收入US$226万，毛利率73.5%，回收期2–3年，IRR约25–35%。成熟期每年可贡献净利约US$2,600万——约相当于FY2025集团归母溢利的23%——而占用资本仅为资产负债表的0.17%。

📊 The Payoff: Closing the One Missing Link

📊 回报：补上唯一缺失的一环

Kuok Group already controls agribusiness processing and trading (Wilmar, US$70.4bn revenue), distribution in 59 countries (KLN), and 106 hotels of F&B demand (Shangri-La). What it does not own is the plant. Adding tissue-culture and aeroponic propagation turns six independent businesses into one vertically integrated coffee platform — a strategic position no peer can replicate by cheque alone — while lifting blended gross margin, adding a non-RevPAR revenue stream and creating the carbon and traceability data asset that reprices US$7.65bn of interest-bearing debt through green financing.

郭氏集团已掌控农业加工与贸易（丰益国际，营收704亿美元）、覆盖59国的物流网络（嘉里物流）与106家酒店的餐饮需求（香格里拉）。它唯一不拥有的，是那株植物。引入组培与雾培育苗，可把六项独立业务整合为一个垂直一体化的咖啡平台——这是任何同业无法仅靠资金复制的战略位置——同时提升整体毛利率、增加一条与RevPAR无关的收入流，并生成可用以通过绿色融资重定价76.5亿美元有息债务的碳与溯源数据资产。

The Kuok Group Portfolio — and the Chain It Is Missing郭氏集团业务组合 —— 以及它所缺失的那一环

🏢 Properties · 4 platforms

🏢 物业 · 4个平台

Kerry Properties (0083.HK) — HK & Mainland developer, ~US$2.9bn market cap; Allgreen Properties (Singapore, private); Shang Properties (Philippines, listed); PPB Group – Property Division (Malaysia). Land and podium space is the raw material for greenhouse siting.

嘉里建设（0083.HK）——香港及内地开发商，市值约29亿美元；Allgreen Properties（新加坡，私人）；Shang Properties（菲律宾，上市）；PPB集团地产分部（马来西亚）。土地与裙楼空间是温室选址的原始资源。

🏨 Hospitality · Shangri-La Group

🏨 酒店 · 香格里拉集团

Shangri-La Asia (00069.HK / SGX: S07), listed 1993. 106 hotels under management across four brands — Shangri-La, JEN, Kerry Hotels, Traders — plus the new ultra-luxury Shangri-La Signatures. 87 owned/leased hotels, 42,500 rooms. This is the captive coffee market.

香格里拉亚洲（00069.HK / 新交所：S07），1993年上市。管理106家酒店，涵盖香格里拉、JEN、嘉里酒店、盛贸四大品牌，以及全新超奢华品牌香格里拉心玺。自有／租赁酒店87家、约4.25万间客房。这就是内嵌的咖啡市场。

🌾 Food & Agribusiness · Wilmar · FFM

🌾 食品与农业 · 丰益国际 · FFM

Wilmar International (SGX: F34) — the Group's largest shareholding; FY2025 revenue US$70.4bn, net profit US$1.41bn, EBITDA US$4.27bn. FFM Berhad (Malaysia, flour & animal feed, est. 1962). Commodity sourcing, processing and consumer-goods distribution for the packaged-coffee route.

丰益国际（新交所：F34）——集团最大持股；FY2025营收704亿美元、净利14.1亿美元、EBITDA 42.7亿美元。FFM Berhad（马来西亚，面粉与饲料，成立于1962年）。为包装咖啡提供大宗采购、加工与快消分销通路。

🚢 Logistics · KLN

🚢 物流 · 嘉里物流

KLN Logistics Group (00636.HK), founded 1981 as Kerry Godown — FY2025 continuing-operations revenue above HK$56bn, operations across 59 countries and territories. Cold-chain and cross-border capability is the distribution layer for seedlings and green beans.

嘉里物流（00636.HK），1981年以Kerry Godown起家——FY2025持续经营业务收入超560亿港元，业务覆盖59个国家和地区。冷链与跨境能力构成种苗与咖啡生豆的分销层。

⚓ Maritime · PCL · POSH · PaxOcean · McPEC

⚓ 海事 · PCL · POSH · PaxOcean · McPEC

Pacific Carriers (1973), PACC Offshore Services (2006), PaxOcean shipyards (2007) and McPEC marine engineering — bulk shipping capacity and engineering discipline transferable to controlled-environment agriculture modules.

太平洋航运（1973）、POSH离岸服务（2006）、PaxOcean船厂（2007）与McPEC海事工程——散货运输能力与工程化能力可迁移至可控环境农业模块。

🖥 Digital Infrastructure · K2 Strategic

🖥 数字基础设施 · K2 Strategic

K2 Strategic, led by the third generation, builds data centres. Applied to the farm network, the same telemetry and edge-compute stack turns every greenhouse into an instrumented node — producing the per-seedling carbon and yield data that becomes a reportable ESG asset.

K2 Strategic 由家族第三代主导，建设数据中心。将其遥测与边缘计算栈应用于农场网络，可把每座温室变为可计量节点——产出每株咖啡的碳足迹与产量数据，形成可披露的ESG资产。
