Revenue Fully Recovered — Profit and Returns Are Not

收入已完全修复 —— 盈利与回报尚未

FY2016–FY2018 were the high-water mark: revenue grew 22.5% to US$2.52bn and attributable profit rose 82% to US$193m, with associate contributions and investment-property fair-value gains doing the heavy lifting. Then COVID cut FY2020 revenue 57.5% overnight to US$1.03bn and three consecutive loss years lost US$9.10bn. Revenue has since recovered to US$2.23bn — 89% of the FY2018 peak — but attributable profit has fallen three straight years to US$112m, because the non-operating tailwind that inflated the good years has reversed. Group ROE, 3.1% at the FY2018 peak, is now 2.1% on a US$14.4bn asset base.

FY2016–FY2018是十年高点：营收增长22.5%至25.2亿美元，归母溢利增长82%至1.93亿美元，其中联营公司贡献与投资物业公平值收益充当主力。随后疫情令FY2020收入一夜暴跌57.5%至10.3亿美元，三年连亏合计91.0亿美元。此后收入修复至22.3亿美元——为FY2018峰值的89%——但归母溢利连续三年下滑至1.12亿美元，原因正是当年推高好年份的非营运顺风已反转。集团ROE在FY2018峰值时为3.1%，如今在144亿美元资产基数上仅为2.1%。

Analyst Read分析师解读

A decade whose cumulative profit was US$1.56bn

十年累计利润仅15.6亿美元

US$194.9bn of revenue produced US$1.56bn of cumulative attributable profit — 0.8% across the cycle. Strip out the four years to FY2019 and the numbers deteriorate sharply: FY2020–FY2025 generated US$103.0bn of revenue and a cumulative attributable loss of US$4.53bn. This is the arithmetic behind a 0.34× book valuation: the market is not disputing the assets, it is disputing the return they generate.

1,949亿美元营收只产生15.6亿美元累计归母溢利——全周期0.8%。剔除截至FY2019的四年后数字急剧恶化：FY2020–FY2025录得1,030亿美元营收，累计归母却为亏损45.3亿美元。这正是0.34倍账面估值的算术基础：市场质疑的不是资产，而是资产产生的回报。

Non-operating items are the entire FY2025 story

FY2025的全部问题都在非营运项目

Consolidated revenue +2.2%, EBITDA +3.4%, operating PATMI +6.0%, free cash flow +14.8%. Every operating line improved. The 30.4% drop in attributable profit is the non-operating swing: investment-property fair-value gains fell from US$74.9m to US$17.9m and a UK hotel impairment of US$30.4m was taken. Judge this company on operating PATMI and cash flow; the headline is accounting noise on top of a genuinely improving core.

综合收入+2.2%、EBITDA +3.4%、营运归母溢利+6.0%、自由现金流+14.8%。每一项营运指标都在改善。归母溢利下滑30.4%完全来自非营运项波动：投资物业公平值收益从7,490万降至1,790万美元，另计提3,040万美元英国酒店减值。评估这家公司应看营运溢利与现金流；表观数字是叠加在真实改善核心之上的会计噪音。

One asset class, three satellites

一个资产类别，三块卫星业务

Hotel Properties is 88.4% of revenue; hotel management and related services contributes 4.4%, investment properties 6.2%, property development 0.4%. Group performance is therefore hostage to RevPAR, ADR and hotel-property fair values — cyclically correlated with tourism flows, the mainland-traveller spending shift and geopolitics (the Group cites Middle East tension as a near-term risk). There is no uncorrelated earnings stream inside the perimeter.

酒店物业占营收88.4%，酒店管理及相关服务4.4%、投资物业6.2%、物业发展0.4%。集团业绩因此高度受制于RevPAR、平均房价与酒店物业公平值——与旅游流量、内地消费迁移及地缘政治周期高度相关（集团已就中东局势提示短期风险）。业务边界之内不存在一条不相关的盈利流。
